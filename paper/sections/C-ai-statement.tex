\section{Statement on the use of AI}\label{app:ai}

\emph{How this work was produced.} The construction, the proofs and the certificate code in this paper were developed with
extensive use of AI agents (Anthropic's Claude models), working as a team under the direction of the author. The agents proposed
constructions, wrote and revised proofs, wrote the computer-assisted certificates, and drafted this manuscript.

\emph{How it has been checked.} Every review so far has been carried out by AI agents. Two component referees checked the individual
parts of the argument, in several rounds, and an independent end-to-end referee then read the complete proof. That referee found no
blocking error, found a number of repairable gaps, all of which were repaired, and finally judged that the argument proves
Theorem~\ref{thm:A} for $\theta\in[\tfrac14,\tfrac12-10^{-4}]$. The reports are included in the \texttt{review/} directory of the
repository that accompanies this paper. The certificates of Appendix~\ref{app:cert} were re-run independently during that review.

\begingroup\sloppy
\emph{Corrections found while writing.} Turning the refereed working draft into this paper, a final AI consistency check of
the paper against that draft (report \texttt{review/paper-check.md}), and reports received after that check surfaced slips in the
refereed draft (in constants, missing
terms, statements broader than their proofs, and individual proof steps). The paper corrects seventy-two of them; each is logged,
with its justification, in the file \texttt{CORRECTIONS-vs-v3.md} of the repository, which lists every deviation of this paper
from the refereed draft. None of them changes any conclusion. Entries 31--72 of the log were reported after the AI checks were complete (twenty-five of them
by a reader of the first public version) or were found while repairing or re-checking those; their repairs have been checked only
by targeted AI re-checks, whose reports \texttt{R4.md}, \texttt{R5-pil-repair.md}, \texttt{R7-margin-var.md},
\texttt{R8-cert-repro.md}, \texttt{R9-cond-check-55.md}, \texttt{R9b-pil-o3b-55.md}, \texttt{R10-lp-transfer.md}, \texttt{R11-absorption.md}, \texttt{R12-explicit-68.md} and \texttt{R13-explicit-69.md} are in the directory
\texttt{review/recheck/}. Further fixes were made, during the same checks, to proofs that were newly written out for this
paper.\par\endgroup

\emph{A gap found and closed after publication.} Correction 48 concerns the label sum of the Pivot-Integration Lemma
(Lemma~\ref{lem:pil}). Its first repair proved the lemma for one configuration type. Two later independent AI checks
(\texttt{review/recheck/R6-cond-check.md} and \texttt{review/recheck/R6b-pil-o3b.md}) found that Theorem~\ref{thm:R} and
the Cover Bound (Proposition~\ref{prop:cover}) use the bound summed over all free-prime structures, and that the per-type
repair does not cover that sum. For a time, therefore, the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:A} was incomplete. Correction 55
replaces the block bound by one that sums over every block content (Lemma~\ref{lem:fac}\,(S3)). Theorem~\ref{thm:R} then
carries an extra factor $k^k/k!\le e^k$, and the Cover Bound carries factors absorbed by the rows outside the cover; no
conclusion changes. Correction 55 was checked by two further independent adversarial AI checks
(\texttt{review/recheck/R9-cond-check-55.md} and \texttt{review/recheck/R9b-pil-o3b-55.md}), one of them by the agent
that found the gap. Like every other repair here, it has not been checked by a human.

\emph{What has not been done.} No human expert has verified the proof. The author takes responsibility for the manuscript, and
welcomes scrutiny, corrections and counterexamples.

\emph{Material checked only by AI checks made for this paper.} The proofs below were written out in full for this paper. In the
form given here they have not been seen by the earlier referees. They have been checked only by AI checks made for this paper: the
consistency check \texttt{review/paper-check.md}, and, for text rewritten after that check reported, the targeted re-checks in
\texttt{review/recheck/}.
\begin{itemize}
\item in the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:bs}: the induction slack $\Sigma$ of Step~5 (\eqref{eq:bs-Sigma}), region Z1, and the
remainder lemma for $\ell\ge10^{18}$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:bs-remainder});
\item the local prime number theorem, Theorem~\ref{thm:lp}, including Landau's lemma and the explicit bound $G(L)$ and its range in
part (iii);
\item the Kantorovich--Rubinstein bounds, Theorems~\ref{thm:kr} and~\ref{thm:kr-random};
\item Proposition~\ref{prop:true-kernels} and Lemmas~\ref{lem:tau-near} and~\ref{lem:S};
\item the three ranges of the $X_0$-band bound (Proposition~\ref{prop:xb-ranges}) and Lemma~\ref{lem:si};
\item the base case of the induction in the proof of Lemma~\ref{lem:smooth-v};
\item the proof of the direction (b)$\Rightarrow$(a) in Remark~\ref{rem:equiv}, and the proof of Lemma~\ref{lem:reduction}(iii);
\item the bookkeeping of the Pivot-Integration Lemma, Lemma~\ref{lem:pil}, with the Euler chains (S3) of Lemma~\ref{lem:fac};
\item the analytic proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:caplw}, Steps 0--6;
\item the explicit constants introduced by corrections \#20--\#22 of the log: the landing windows and $K_{\mathrm{loc}}$ of
Corollary~\ref{cor:localcount}, the constants in Step~5 of the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:caplw}, in step (v) of the proof of

\end{itemize}
The same holds for the other corrections logged in \texttt{CORRECTIONS-vs-v3.md}.

A reader who wishes to check the proof with limited time might start with these two lists.
