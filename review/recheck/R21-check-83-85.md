## #83: X_q restricted to jittered latest primes

### Verified
1. **C_jit identity.** Rational primes are decided at t(p) = p < X₀, and jittered primes at slots j ≥ X₀ with s_j > X₀. So a
   rational latest prime means every prime of n is rational: n ∈ 𝒟 is rigid. An element with a jittered prime and a rational
   latest prime is impossible. Hence C_jit(I) = Σ over jittered q of (X^{(1)}_q + X^{(2)}_q), exactly.
2. **Excess identity.** The composites split into three disjoint classes: rigid, 𝒜 (latest prime jittered, so 𝒜 ∩ 𝒟 = ∅),
   and C_jit. Nothing falls outside them.
3. **Comp^{(2)}.** The new formula is exactly Σ_q E[X^{(2)}_q | F_{q−}]. The old formula was only an upper bound for it, so
   Mart^{(2)} would have been a supermartingale, not a martingale. Every consumer uses Comp^{(2)} as an upper bound. The ν and
   E(I) identities stay exact for any Comp^{(2)}, because Mart^{(2)} := C^{(≥2)} − Comp^{(2)} by definition.
4. **Freedman for E2(b).** It closes, with count ≤ λ. The bound a_max ≤ L/Λ + 1 needs q ≥ X₀⁻.
5. **Corollary increment restricted to jittered q.** Every consumer reads only jittered q.

### The reported counterexample does not apply
X^{(2)}_p needs p to be the latest prime of n. For a rational p, that means p is the largest prime factor of n. Small-scale
check:

| Window | Primes p with p² ≤ \|I\| | Of those, p is the largest prime of some n ∈ I with p² \| n |
|---|---|---|
| [10¹³, 10¹³ + 2·10⁵) | 86 | 15 |
| [10¹², 10¹² + 2·10⁵) | 86 | 22 |

At Λ ≥ 10⁸, the expected count per window is about exp(1430 − 57000). So for rational q, (b) is unproved, not shown false.
The repair is right either way: the rational part is never needed.
