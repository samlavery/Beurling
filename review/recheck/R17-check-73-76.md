## 3. Outside the scope of #73/#76 (became correction #77)
02 l.203ff (the choice of ϑ_s) says that the deterministic jump levels form a countable set. This is false once Abs or R
is active. Above the Abs onset, T(d−) at a rigid d is random, and its law depends on ϑ_s through the slots, so the
countability argument is really a fixed-point condition. In the tie case T(d−) = j + ϑ_s, the slot J_j is empty. This is
unrelated to the causality of γ_k.
