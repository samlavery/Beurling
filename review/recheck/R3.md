## 3. Priority 1: the PIL/(S3) Euler-chain rewrite (I5, #27), A-rankfull.tex:163-327

1. **Eu_s is indexed over slots < s.** RESOLVED. Eu_s(s) is the product over realised primes "decided before s", i.e.
   slot < s, with the rational primes < X₀ included (l.264-268). This matches the (F3′) second case (05-queue.tex:111-112).
   - (S3)(a) (l.306-308): a cofactor with primes of slot in (s_{t+1}, s_t) is a product of primes of slot in
     [s_{t+1}, s_t). Its harmonic sum is ≤ Eu_s(s_t)/Eu_s(s_{t+1}). This is a superset bound: it also counts the pivots of
     P_{t+1} and any non-configuration primes in that range. Every factor is ≥ 1, so it is valid.

2. **The block below each pivot is bounded inside the conditional expectation at s_p, so Eu_s(s_p) cancels before the

     𝓕_{s_t−}-measurable.
   - The product (1/Eu_s(s_t))·(Eu_s(s_t)/Eu_s(s_{t+1})) = 1/Eu_s(s_{t+1}) is an exact identity.
   - 1/Eu_s(s_{t+1}) depends only on the label s_{t+1} and on primes of slot < s_{t+1}. These are earlier than every

       defined, since the early-block range "slot < s_T" refers to a summed label.

         for the late blocks. Then (1/Eu_s(s_T))·Eu_s(s_T) = 1 per row.
